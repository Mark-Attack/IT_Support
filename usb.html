    • Universal Serial Bus (USB)

    • USB is the standard used to connect peripherals to a computer
    • USB cables come in different types



    • Type A used to connect devices to a PC or laptop (mouse, keyboard, thumb drive)
    • Type B is mostly used for printers
    • Mini was used on older smartphones and digital cameras (rarely used nowadays)
    • Micro A is used in newer smartphones and digital cameras (replaced the Mini)
    • Micro B used to carry data and supply power to devices (E.g. External hard drives)
    • Type C initially used USB 3.1 standards (10Gbps). All new smartphones and tablets use this type. Aims to replace the Micro A for devices.


    Type A Ports





    • “Always on” ports are usually yellow but can be orange or red
    • They can also be determined by a lightning bolt or a battery icon next to the port
    • These ports are always providing power, even if the pc I turned off
    • They are used mainly to charge a device (smartphone, tablets, etc)
