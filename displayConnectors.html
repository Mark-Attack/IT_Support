    Video Graphics Array (VGA)


    • Old analog video connector
    • Does NOT support audio
    • Usually color-coded blue and has screws on either side


    Digital Visual Interface (DVI)



    • Can carry both analog and digital signals
    • Still does NOT support audio
    • Comes in many variations, each having the same form factor but different pins




    • DVI-A: only supports analog video outputs (no digital support)
    • DVI-D Single Link: only supports digital video outputs (no Analog support)
    • DVI-I Single Link: supports both analog and digital outputs

    • The Dual Link connectors (DVI-I and DVI-D) carry extra pins and as a result supports higher resolutions
    • However, the supported outputs are the same as their single link versions


    High Definition Media Interface (HDMI)


    • designed to transmit both high-quality digital audio and video signals
    • Different versions were released throughout the years that supports higher output resolutions
    • The version is usually printed on the cable




    Display Port



    • Transmits high quality digital video and audio like HDMI
    • It supports higher bandwidth which means higher refresh rates at high resolutions
    • Allows daisy-chaining multiple monitors with a feature called multi-stream transport (MST)
    • This allows one port to power multiple monitors.

    What is DSC?

    Display Stream Compression (DSC) is a technology that compresses the video signal a little so the cable can send higher resolutions and higher refresh rates, like 4K 144Hz, even if the cable normally wouldn't have enough bandwidth.
